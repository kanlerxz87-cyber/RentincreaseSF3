Timing and banking

Usually the first annual increase can take effect 12 months after the tenancy begins, and subsequent increases are at least 12 months apart. A changed effective date can reset the anniversary. A continuing tenant and a changed roommate do not automatically establish a new tenancy or permit an extra charge; the actual documents and facts must be reviewed.
