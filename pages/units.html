Why the difference between the two units is legally significant

The gap between Unit A (3.0%) and Unit B (13.1% or more) is not a choice. It is the arithmetic consequence of two objectively documented facts — how long each tenancy has run and how many published annual rates were skipped — applied through a regulation that treats every unit identically. That is precisely the kind of facially neutral, statute-driven, document-supported justification that answers a discrimination inquiry. It also explains why applying the same percentage to both units would be unlawful: it would push Unit A above its ceiling. See One unit or both?
