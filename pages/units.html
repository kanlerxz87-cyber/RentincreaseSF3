Reasonable & normal benchmark

BenchmarkWhat \"reasonable\" means hereSource
Rent-controlled ceilingThe Rent Board's published annual allowable % — currently 1.4%–1.6% — is the legal ceiling and the market notion of \"normal\" for these units. SF's 8-year average is ~1.96%; no official ZIP-level market rent exists to quote.sf.gov — rent increases · Form 571 PDF
AB 1482 state backstop5% + CPI, max 10% (SF area 8.8% for Aug 1, 2026–Jul 31, 2027) — but exempt because a stricter local ordinance already covers these units. The state figure is not the lawful amount to charge here.sf.gov — AB 1482 · Civ. Code §1947.12
Banked catch-upLawful but not \"normal\" — it is the practice tenant advocates and the Board of Supervisors are actively targeting; it draws petitions and may soon be capped at 10%/yr. Phasing (e.g., 10% now, remainder later) is more defensible.sf.gov — banking · File #261020 (pending)
