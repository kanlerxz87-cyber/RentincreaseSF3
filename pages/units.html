Why this is a controlled continuation and not a market reset: under Administrative Code § 37.3(d)(2)(B) and Rent Board Rule § 6.14, Costa-Hawkins / § 6.14 unlimited increases do not apply to partial changes in occupancy while an original occupant (Tenant A) remains in lawful possession, and under § 37.3(a)(11)(A) adding an occupant (Tenant B) never authorises an extra rent charge. If instead the 1 April 2026 lease is treated as restarting the 12-month anniversary clock, the first increase would fall on 1 April 2027 — both dates are on the Flagged page (FR-02).
