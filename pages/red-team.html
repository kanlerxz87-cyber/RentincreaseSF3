Red team: would this survive a San Francisco tenant lawyer?

San Francisco is the most tenant-protective jurisdiction in California. A tenant here can obtain free legal counsel, file a free petition with the Rent Board, and have an Administrative Law Judge determine the lawful rent from the complete rent history. This page assumes that happens and works backwards from it.

The adversary

The Rent Board publishes an official referral listing of free tenant-side services, including Bay Area Legal Aid, Housing Rights Committee of San Francisco, Eviction Defense Collaborative, SF Tenants Union, Causa Justa :: Just Cause, Tenderloin Housing Clinic, Open Door Legal, Advancing Justice-Asian Law Caucus, Legal Assistance to the Elderly, Chinatown CDC and the SRO Collaboratives. The Board also states that no lawyer is needed to file a petition, and that it cannot refer to individual attorneys. Assume the tenant is represented and is competent. Source: S15 · S14

Verdict

Conditional pass

On the facts as supplied, the recommended increases are defensible: each is the published annual allowable increase plus properly matured banked amounts, computed on base rent, added rather than compounded, truncated rather than rounded, served with the correct notice period, landing on the existing anniversary date, and untainted by any occupancy-based, algorithmic, retaliatory or discriminatory motive. The differential treatment of the two units is produced by the Ordinance itself and is fully documented.

Three conditions must be satisfied first. They are not formalities — each can defeat an otherwise correct increase:

  1. Confirm a current rent increase license on the effective date for each unit. Without it the entire increase is null and void regardless of the arithmetic. This is the highest-residual-risk item in the file.
  2. Confirm the banking maturity rule with a Rent Board counselor, because two official sf.gov pages give different answers (12 versus 24 months). If the 24-month reading were applied, part of Unit B’s 13.1% would fall away — which is a reason to consider a partial increase that is defensible under either reading.
  3. Assemble the rent history, ledger and payment records, and confirm base-rent composition and the building’s construction date and unit type.

And one drafting caution: Unit B’s total already exceeds 10%, so the 90-day clock is fixed regardless of what the unpublished 2027-28 annual rate turns out to be. Serve by March 3, 2027, or February 26, 2027 if mailing.

If a petition arrives

  1. Do not ignore it. The Rent Board usually resolves matters by mediation, otherwise by arbitration before an Administrative Law Judge. Filing is free for the tenant. Source: S14
  2. File Form 302 (Landlord's Response to Tenant Petition) within the stated time. Source: S19
  3. Bring the worksheet, the rent history, the notice, the proof of service and the license confirmation.
  4. Remember the jurisdictional split: the Rent Board decides the amount of rent. Harassment, discrimination, retaliation and security deposits are decided in court or another forum. Source: S14 · S33 · S34
  5. A good-faith overage within half of one percent of the prior base rent may be readjusted rather than voided. Source: S5
  6. Free landlord counselling is available on 415-252-4600, weekdays 9am-12pm and 1pm-4pm. Source: S7
