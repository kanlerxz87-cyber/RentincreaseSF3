Sources deliberately NOT used

Third-party rent sites were checked and rejected

Several widely circulated third-party pages disagree with sf.gov on the basics. They were read, compared and excluded; none is cited anywhere on this site. Examples of what they got wrong: a ‘SF rent control calculator’ states the current allowable increase is 1.7% when the official rate is 1.6%; a brokerage’s tenant guide states a 90-day notice applies to increases ‘of 10% or more’ when the statute sets the trigger at greater than 10%; another guide states a 60-day notice period that appears nowhere in Civ. Code Sec. 827. The conflict is recorded as flags F2 and F3.

The resolution rule applied throughout: where a third-party source conflicts with sf.gov, leginfo.legislature.ca.gov, dir.ca.gov, justice.gov, calcivilrights.ca.gov, sec.gov, lao.ca.gov or the official source controls and the conflict is flagged.
