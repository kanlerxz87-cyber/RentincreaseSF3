Reproducing the verification

The site is a static build. The checks that gate publication are:

  • python3 tools/verify_core_site.py — every internal link and anchor resolves; every table row links an official domain; privacy scan; arithmetic assertions; no remote scripts.
  • Manual re-read of each cited page on 10 Nov 2026, with the quoted wording compared to the page text.
  • Cross-checks: 1.6% appears on both the Rent Board page and document 571; 1.4% appears on document 571 and in the Rent Board's prior-year example; 8.8% appears on the Attorney General's regional table.
