How the verification was done

  • Each figure was read from the publisher's page on 2026-11-10 and transcribed; no figure was carried over from an earlier edition without being re-read.
  • Every table row in this edition is machine-checked for an official link or a flag marker, and every internal link and anchor is checked against the filesystem.
  • Arithmetic is recomputed in the build from the published percentages and the owner's base rents, to the cent, using addition rather than compounding.
  • An anonymisation sweep runs over every page: no tenant names or initials, no street address, no unit identifiers that reveal location, and no ZIP code.
