Suggestions & decision paths

A dedicated place to review suggestions and choose a path before anything is implemented. Status labels are honest: “Implemented” means it already exists on this site; “Proposed” means nothing has been done — a reviewer picks what to adopt. Research pass: 13 November 2026.

How to use this page

  1. Read the suggestions in Table 1 and mark each one: adopt, adapt or reject. Nothing marked “Proposed” has been implemented anywhere on this site.
  2. Close the open questions first — the paths below assume the licence, rent history and anniversary questions are answered: Questions, answers & missing information.
  3. Choose one path in Table 2, record the choice and the reason in a dated note, then execute through Licence & process (licence → calculate → notice → serve).
  4. Re-check the pending ordinance (File #261020) immediately before serving any banked amount.
