One unit or both?

Different outcomes may be justified by verifiable differences in timing, lawful base rents or histories. Do not base a decision on protected characteristics or protected complaints; assess potential discriminatory effect and retaliation. A neutral, documented approach is prudent, not a guarantee against a claim. Official source ↗

Large and individual owners

The same San Francisco annual and license rules apply to a covered unit irrespective of whether the owner is an individual or company. An internal schedule and standardized notices are sensible management practices, but no official comparison establishing what most local corporate or individual landlords actually do was verified; do not treat a maximum as a normal market average. Official source ↗ Official source ↗

Tenant-advocate stress test

Before service, check license status on the effective date, coverage exceptions, base rent, anniversary, banking evidence, notice contents and service, protected activity and consistent criteria. A tenant may file a Rent Board petition without a filing fee. Free help for tenants with eviction court papers is available through the Eviction Defense Collaborative; other services have differing eligibility.
