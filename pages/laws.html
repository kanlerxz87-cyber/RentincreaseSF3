Which law actually governs the amount?

QuestionAnswerSource
Is the federal government capping my rent?No. No federal rent-control statute exists. Federal law that does govern increase decisions is fair-housing non-discrimination.42 U.S.C. §3604
Does California AB 1482 cap me at 8.8%?No, for these units. AB 1482 (5% + CPI, max 10%) exempts housing already under a stricter local ordinance that restricts increases to a lower amount — that's SF's 1.4%/1.6%. If the local cap did not apply, the 8.8% would.Civ. Code §1947.12(d)(3) · sf.gov — AB 1482
So what is my lawful ceiling?The Rent Board's annual allowable increase: 1.4% / 1.6%, plus any correctly calculated banking. That's the figure a tenant petition judge will apply.sf.gov — rent increases · sf.gov — Tenant Petitions
