Methodology & verification

This page states exactly how each number was obtained, what counts as verified, what was left blank and why, and how to reproduce every figure. The governing principle applied throughout: if a value cannot be confirmed from an official source, it is left blank and flagged. Nothing is estimated, extrapolated or forecast.

1. What “verified” means here

Verification levels used on this site
Label Meaning
Verified The value was read directly off a document published by an official body (a .gov publisher, the California Legislature, or the U.S. SEC) during research on November 6, 2026, and that document is linked on the row.
Official URL – CI-checked The URL is an official-source URL recorded in the register and its availability is confirmed by the automated link check, but its full text was not read line by line for this project. Such a source is never the sole support for a numeric value.
Unverified – flagged The value could not be confirmed from an official source. It is left blank on the site and appears in the flag register.
Not published The responsible agency had not published the value as of the analysis date. Left blank; never forecast.
Not applicable No official source sets this value because the law does not provide for one (for example, a federal rent ceiling).

2. Rules applied to every calculation

The eight rules the calculator implements, each tied to an official source
Rule What it requires Official source Status
R1 Rent may be increased only once every 12 months. The first increase is available 12 months after the lease began; the effective date of any increase becomes the new anniversary date. Source: S3 · S16 Verified
R2 A skipped annual increase ‘banks’ only once a full 12 months has elapsed from the date it could first have been imposed. Rules & Regs Sec. 4.12(a). Source: S5 · S4 Verified
R3 Banked percentages are added, never compounded, and never rounded up. Total increase = (sum of matured banked percentages) + (the annual rate in effect on the effective date), applied to base rent. Source: S5 · S4 · S3 Verified
R4 Moving the increase off the anniversary date permanently forfeits any skipped rate that had not yet completed its 12 months. Source: S3 Verified
R5 30 days’ written notice where the total increase is 10% or less; 90 days where it is more than 10% (measured against the rent charged at any time in the prior 12 months, alone or combined). Add 5 days if the notice is mailed. Source: S23 · S3 · S4 Verified
R6 A current rent increase license must be on file on the effective date, or the entire annual or banked increase is null and void. Source: S8 · S9 · S6 Verified
R7 No increase may be based on the addition or replacement of an occupant, even if the lease purports to allow it. Source: S11 · S6 · S12 Verified
R8 Amounts are truncated to the cent and never rounded up to the next dollar. Source: S3 · S17 Verified

5. Privacy method

Anonymisation applied to everything published

Unit 1 is published as Unit A; Unit 2 as Unit B. Tenants are published as pseudonymous identifiers (T-A1 to T-A4, T-B1 to T-B2). Only role changes are shown — original tenant, departing tenant, added occupant — never a name. All dates and all dollar amounts are preserved exactly as supplied. No street address was supplied and none is published; the location appears only as ‘San Francisco, CA 94122 (Outer Sunset)’. The pseudonym-to-identity mapping is held by the owner and does not exist anywhere in this repository.

This matters beyond preference: information an owner files in the Rent Board Housing Inventory is public, including base rent in $250 increments. Source: S8

6. Reproducing every figure

python3 tools/calc.py        # runs the 4 official self-tests, then prints every published number
python3 tools/build_site.py  # regenerates the site from data/_calc.json
python3 tools/check_links.py # verifies every internal and external link

tools/calc.py aborts with a non-zero exit status if any self-test fails, so a broken calculation can never be published.

7. Known limits of this analysis

  • This is not legal advice. It is a documented, source-linked analysis intended to be handed to counsel or to a Rent Board counselor for confirmation.
  • Coverage is presumed from an owner-reported 1940 construction year, not verified against a certificate of occupancy (flag F9).
  • The rent increase license status is unknown (flag F4) and is a hard precondition to any enforceable increase.
  • The annual rate for the period beginning March 1, 2027 had not been published (flag F10); Unit B’s total is therefore published as “13.1% + unpublished”.
  • The full text of Rent Ordinance Sec. 37.8 could not be parsed from the compiled PDF, so the refund look-back period for a null-and-void increase is not stated (flag F15).
