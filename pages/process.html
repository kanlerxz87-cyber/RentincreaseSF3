Reasons and exceptions

An ordinary annual adjustment can be described accurately as the Rent Board’s annual allowable increase. Do not claim a cost-based passthrough or approval without the necessary facts. Capital improvements have separate rules: the landlord must file a petition before serving a capital-improvement notice, and tenants need not pay that portion before final approval. Official source ↗

The City’s materials describe inventory reporting and a license rather than a separate advance approval petition for ordinary annual/banked increases; they do not establish a general requirement to send the Rent Board a copy of every ordinary increase notice. Different filings can apply to other types of notice. Official source ↗

Other requirements are separate

The Rent Board fee is separate from free Housing Inventory reporting; check whether a fee or exemption applies. Do not confuse a rent-increase license with any separate business registration requirements. Housing Inventory ↗ · Rent Board fee ↗
