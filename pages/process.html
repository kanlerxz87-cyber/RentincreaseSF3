Process and notice

TopicFinding / actionSource
1. Verify factsConfirm coverage, rent history, anniversary, lease terms, voucher status and any restrictions before calculating.Official source
2. Report and licenseFile in the Housing Inventory; keep a current license on file before the annual or banked increase takes effect. The city says the license need not be included with the notice.Official source
3. CalculateUse the applicable effective-date rate and verify any banked increases and their dates. Annual or banked increases do not require a petition; other types of increases have separate rules.Official source
4. Serve noticeServe a written notice consistent with Civil Code § 827 and lease requirements. At most 10% requires at least 30 days; over 10% requires at least 90 days, considering increases in the preceding 12 months. Allow additional time where required by method of service.Official source
5. Keep records and seek reviewRetain calculations, proof of service and a neutral rationale. The Rent Board offers counseling; tenant resources are available. No review can guarantee against a challenge.Official source
