FR-30Notice-content rules carry a safe harbour the site had not publishedVerified — added 11 Nov 2026Rule § 4.12(b) requires the landlord to inform the tenant, on or before the date legal notice is given, which portion of the increase is banked and the dates on which the banked amount is based — but adds: “provided, however, that failure to include such information shall not render the increase null and void.” Separately, Rule § 4.10(b) provides that a rent increase which does not conform to § 4.10(a) “shall render the entire rent increase null and void, unless the amount requested equals no more than the allowable annual and banked rent increase(s)”, with a readjustment where a good-faith increase exceeds the limit by no more than one-half of one percent of the prior base rent.
