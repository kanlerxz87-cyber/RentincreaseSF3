Bottom line

A "reasonable and normal" increase for these units is exactly the Rent Board's published annual allowable increase — nothing more — delivered with proper written notice, applied uniformly to both units, and calculated on base rent only. Anything above the cap requires a Rent Board petition (e.g., capital improvements / operating costs) and should be reviewed by a tenant-rights attorney before being served.
