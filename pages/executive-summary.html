Executive summary

San Francisco · Anonymized public edition

1 · License and Rent Board reporting

Yes. Individual owners are not exempt. Report each residential unit annually in the Rent Board Housing Inventory. A rent-increase license is generated for non-owner-occupied units; it must be current on the effective date of an annual or banked increase. Obtain it before serving notice as a prudent precaution; the City specifies the legal deadline as the effective date, not necessarily the service date. A license does not immunize an increase from challenge. SF Housing Inventory

Reporting is free and due March 1 each year. Late reporting can still generate a license. The City says inventory submissions contain public information, so do not mistake the portal for a private repository. Official source ↗

The Rent Board says ordinary annual/banked increases do not require a separate petition for approval. Reporting and a valid license are still required; do not confuse a petition with inventory reporting. Official source ↗
