How the three layers fit together

Federal

No rent ceiling. The binding federal rules are non-discrimination in rental terms (42 U.S.C. 3604) and antitrust limits on algorithmic rent-setting, which San Francisco has also banned locally. S20 S24 S23

State

Sets the outer limit and the notice mechanics: 5% + CPI capped at 10% under AB 1482, displaced here by the stricter local cap; 30/90-day notice under Civil Code 827; no retaliation under Civil Code 1942.5; no discrimination under Government Code 12955. S16 S21 S15 S18 S19

Local

Sets the number that actually applies: 1.6% for 3/1/2026-2/28/2027, banked years added on top, one increase per 12 months, licence required, base rent only, no rounding, no charge for additional occupants. S1 S3 S6 S2
