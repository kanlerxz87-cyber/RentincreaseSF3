Repository audit and verification limits

The repository already contained four overlapping editions with conflicting anniversary, banking and recommendation language. This integration replaces the landing page and standalone executive summary, adds a single canonical navigation, and labels prior HTML as archived. Historical source registers and coordination logs are retained, not relabeled as newly verified.

Every consolidated data row links an official source. Blank verified-value cells are intentional. Owner inputs and conditional arithmetic are separated from official property facts. Automated checks validate links, row-source domains, decimal arithmetic and privacy patterns; they do not establish legal entitlement or prove every external legacy URL is still live.

External consolidated source pages were retrieved and read through the page-retrieval tool; no blanket external-link certification is made for archived editions. A working URL by itself is not substantive verification.
