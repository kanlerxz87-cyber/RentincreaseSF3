Analysis The broadest “what is normal” answer for a rent-controlled unit is not a market figure; it is the published annual allowance plus whatever that tenancy lawfully banked. A 3.0% increase on Unit 1 is normal because two annual allowances went unused. A 13.1% increase on Unit 2 is also lawful, but it is not “normal” in the sense a reviewer means — it is a seven-year catch-up, and the 90-day notice exists precisely because increases above 10% are treated as materially different. Civ. Code § 827
