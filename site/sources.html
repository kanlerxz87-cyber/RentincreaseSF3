How the verification was done

  1. Each source was retrieved directly from its publisher on 10 November 2026 and read in full, not summarised from a search result.
  2. Each rate was read from Form 571 and cross-checked against the Rent Board's announcement for the same year. The two agreed for every year used.
  3. Each arithmetic result was recomputed in code from the published percentage and the stated base rent, with the result asserted at build time, so a wrong figure fails the build rather than reaching the page.
  4. Each table row is required by an automated check to carry at least one link to an official domain. A row with no official source fails the build.
  5. Where two official pages disagree, both readings are published and the disagreement is listed on Flagged for review instead of being resolved by preference. See F-02.
  6. Values that no official source supports — the ZIP-level benchmark, the comparative corporate-versus- individual data — are left blank and flagged, not filled with an estimate.

Sources deliberately not used

Law-firm summaries, landlord-association guidance, tenant-advocacy explainers and news coverage of the pending ordinance were consulted to identify which questions to ask, but no figure, rule or conclusion in this review rests on any of them. Where such material made a claim, the claim was traced back to the official document before being included.
