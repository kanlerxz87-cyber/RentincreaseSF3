Coverage determination

San Francisco rent control attaches to most residential units in buildings with a certificate of occupancy on or before 13 June 1979, and limits increases during a continuing tenancy; the rent can be reset only once the unit is vacant and no tenant from the prior tenancy remains. Building age is the owner's statement, so the determination below is provisional until the property record is pulled. S5 S13

Why Unit A did not become a new tenancy in April 2026

Vacancy decontrol applies to a new tenancy in which no tenant from the prior tenancy remains in lawful possession (Civ. Code 1947.12(b); Costa-Hawkins, Civ. Code 1954.52). On 1 April 2026 one of the original Unit A tenants was still in place, with a co-tenant added, so the tenancy continued; the rent therefore stayed at its lawful level and did not reset. S16 S17 S13

The addition of the co-tenant is not a rentable event in San Francisco: the ordinance prohibits increases imposed solely because a tenant added an occupant, and states that this applies even where the lease expressly allows a charge for additional occupants. S2 S13
