The lawful ceiling for each unit, anniversary by anniversary, with the arithmetic shown so it can be re-checked line by line.

Rules applied to every line
One increase per 12 months; percentages are added, never compounded; the percentage applied is the one in effect on the effective date of the increase; the calculation is on base rent only; no rounding up to the nearest dollar. S2 S3 S8 S9

Unit A - $3,995.00 base rent, tenancy began 1 Feb 2025

Because the tenancy began on 1 Feb 2025, the earliest possible increase was 1 Feb 2026 (the first anniversary), at the rate then in effect (1.4%). No increase was imposed. By 1 Feb 2027 the unit has gone 24 full months without an increase, which is the Rent Board's condition for treating a skipped year as banked. S2 S3 S9

Timing window

To use the 1.6% rate the increase must take effect on or before 28 Feb 2027. With a 90-day notice, service must happen by 30 Nov 2026 (25 Nov 2026 if mailed). Served on 6 Nov 2026, the earliest lawful effective date is 4 Feb 2027 (9 Feb 2027 if mailed). S15

Waiting instead for the 1 Jun 2027 anniversary would add the 2027-28 annual rate, which the Rent Board will publish by 1 Mar 2027 and which cannot be stated today. S4

If the 10% cap passes

File 261020 would limit combined banked and capital-improvement increases to 10% per year for three years. It is not law on 2026-11-06. Option B2 (10.0%, $315.00) is lawful now and would stay lawful if the cap is enacted; B3 (13.1%) could be reduced. S22

Verification of the arithmetic

  • Percentages taken from the Rent Board's published table, not from memory. S4
  • Each percentage matched to the rate period in effect on that anniversary date. S2 S9
  • Simple addition, no compounding: 1.4 + 1.6 = 3.0; 1.8 + 0.7 + 2.3 + 3.6 + 1.7 + 1.4 + 1.6 = 13.1. S3 S8
  • Dollar amounts are base rent x summed percentage, computed once; cents kept, nothing rounded up. S2
  • Cross-checked against the Rent Board's own multi-year worked example and the 6.7% banked example. S8 S3
