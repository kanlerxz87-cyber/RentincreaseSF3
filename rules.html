What the official guidance supports—and what it does not.

TopicFinding / reviewOfficial source
San FranciscoCovered units generally have one ordinary annual increase every 12 months; the current rate applies to increases effective March 1, 2026 through February 28, 2027. Verify the effective-date rate.Official guidance ↗
CaliforniaFor covered units the Tenant Protection Act generally limits annual increases to 5% plus regional cost of living or 10%, whichever is lower; exemptions and stricter local limits matter. Do not treat the state formula as permission to exceed the local limit.Official guidance ↗
FederalFair housing law and anti-retaliation rules apply where applicable. No general federal rent cap is asserted here; federal program-specific rules may apply and are unverified.Official guidance ↗
Replacement occupantA replacement or additional roommate alone is not a lawful reason for extra rent. With an original occupant reportedly remaining, do not assume vacancy decontrol; actual status, lease and records need review.Official guidance ↗
ReasonsThe annual allowance is a legal mechanism, not a requirement to charge the maximum. Cost increases may motivate a business decision but do not independently authorize extra rent; separate petitions and passthroughs have their own requirements.Official guidance ↗
One or bothThere is no blanket requirement in the cited guidance to increase both units. Apply an objective, documented policy and check each tenancy independently; protected-class discrimination and retaliation are prohibited. Equal treatment alone is not immunity.Official guidance ↗
