The two-line answer

Under SF Rent Ordinance Chapter 37: each unit’s rent may rise once every 12 months by the allowable annual increase (1.6% for increases effective in 2026–27), plus any lawfully “banked” unused increases. Nothing in state or federal law permits more for these units.
Normal & reasonable practice in SF: apply the allowable percentage (and documented banked years, if any) on each unit’s anniversary date, give written 30/90-day notice with all statutory contents, confirm the Housing Inventory license, and treat both units by the same written, objective formula.
