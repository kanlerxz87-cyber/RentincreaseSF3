## S2 - Learn about rent increases in San Francisco

- Publisher: San Francisco Rent Board (sf.gov)
- URL: https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco
- Used for: 60%-of-CPI formula, 12-month rule/anniversary date, rent increase license, base-rent-only rule, no rounding up, 30/90-day notice, prohibition on charging more for additional occupants, multi-year worked examples.
- Passage: > A landlord can impose the first annual increase 12 months after the date the tenant's lease began. ... This is an unlawful rent increase, even if the lease or rental agreement provides for the additional charge.

## S3 - Banked rent increases

- Publisher: San Francisco Rent Board (sf.gov)
- URL: https://www.sf.gov/information--banked-rent-increases
- Used for: Definition of banking; the 24-month condition; no cap on banked amount; banked increases do not expire during a tenancy; partial increases bank the remainder; notice must identify the banked portion and its dates; no compounding.
- Passage: > A skipped increase is only banked if at least 24 full months pass without any rent increase. If less than 24 months pass, the skipped increase is lost and cannot be imposed at a later date. ... There is no limit on how much can be banked. ... Banked increases do not expire during a tenancy.
