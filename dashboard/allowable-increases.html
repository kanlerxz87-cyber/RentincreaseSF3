Allowable annual increases & banking

Official San Francisco Rent Board rates for rent-controlled units. The rate applicable to an increase is the one in effect on the increase's effective date. Rates run March 1 – February 28 each year. The rate is 60% of the increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers in the Bay Area (sf.gov — Learn about rent increases).

Banking rules (saving skipped increases)

RuleDetailSource (official)
What banking isIf a landlord does not impose an allowed increase, it may be saved (“banked”) and added on a later allowed date; banked increases may be combined with a current annual increase.sf.gov — Banked rent increases
Earliest use of a skipped yearA skipped increase is usable only after enough time passes without any rent increase — the Rent Board's Rules (§ 4.12) state a full 12 months must elapse from the date the increase could first have been imposed; the sf.gov guidance states a skipped increase is only banked if at least 24 full months pass without any rent increase (see F-4 — for these two tenancies both formulations give the same result).sf.gov — Banked rent increases; sf.gov — Learn about rent increases (worked examples)
Lost increase on new anniversaryIf an increase is imposed on a date later than the anniversary (creating a new anniversary date), a skipped year that is not yet banked is permanently lost.sf.gov — Learn about rent increases
No compounding / no rounding upBanked percentages are added together, not compounded, and cannot be rounded up; “Rent increases cannot be rounded up to the nearest dollar.”sf.gov — Banked rent increases
How far backBanking allowed only since April 1, 1982; no limit on how much can be banked; banked amounts do not expire during the tenancy; new owners may use them; they do not carry over to a new tenant.sf.gov — Banked rent increases
Notice must itemize bankingThe rent-increase notice should state the dollar amount, percentage, effective date and which part (and which years) is banked; the license must be in place before the effective date.sf.gov — Banked rent increases
No petition neededAnnual and banked increases may be imposed “without filing a petition” at the Rent Board.Rent Board Form 571

Anniversary-date warning (Unit A)

Imposing Unit A's first increase before Feb 1, 2027 creates a new anniversary date and permanently forfeits the skipped 1.4% (the Rent Board's own “new anniversary date” example shows a skipped, not-yet-banked year being lost). Waiting until Feb 1, 2027 captures 1.6% + 1.4% = 3.0%. Source: sf.gov — Learn about rent increases.
