Start here: the Rent Board

Yes. Individual owners are not exempt. Report each residential unit annually in the Rent Board Housing Inventory. A rent-increase license is generated for non-owner-occupied units; it must be current on the effective date of an annual or banked increase. Obtain it before serving notice as a prudent precaution; the City specifies the legal deadline as the effective date, not necessarily the service date. A license does not immunize an increase from challenge. SF Housing Inventory
