The question you asked, answered first

“I am only an individual owner, not a corporation — do I still have to report to the Rent Board and get a licence before I notify tenants of a rent increase?”

Yes. San Francisco requires all owners of residential property to report into the Rent Board Housing Inventory, and the rent increase licence is generated automatically from that report. It is not an application, it is not limited to corporations, and it costs nothing. Reporting began 1 March 2023 for condominiums and buildings with 1–9 units. The annual deadline is 1 March. Without a licence in effect on the effective date of the increase, the increase notice is not enforceable.

Sources: sf.gov — The Rent Board Housing Inventory · sf.gov — Learn about rent increases · S.F. Admin. Code § 37.3 — full step-by-step on License & process.
