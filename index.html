Rent increase review

First: report and obtain the rent increase license

Yes. An individual owner must report residential property in the Rent Board Housing Inventory; a license is generated after reporting a tenant-occupied unit. For annual or banked increases it must be current on the effective date. Reporting for buildings with fewer than 10 units began March 1, 2023. The city does not say it must be obtained before serving notice; obtaining it first is prudent. The license does not prevent a tenant challenge. SF Rent Board Housing Inventory
