Corporate and individual practice

The same law applies to every owner. What differs is whether the filing, the calendar and the records exist — which is what a reviewer actually inspects.

How increases actually get run

There is no separate rulebook for large owners. The same ordinance, the same allowance and the same licence apply to a two-unit building and to a thousand-unit portfolio. What differs is process: whether the filing, the calendar and the documentation are systematised. The rows below separate what the official sources establish from what they do not.

What this means for the two units

The gap between a careful individual owner and a managed portfolio is not permission — it is evidence. A managed owner can produce a licence page, a bank ledger and a notice template on demand. For this building the equivalent file is partly missing: Unit 2's rent history and the licensing history are both unverified. Fixing those two records is more valuable than optimising the percentage, because either one, not the arithmetic, is what would decide a petition.
