Irregularities found in earlier editions on this repository

Recorded here so the corrections are traceable, not to reopen them. Each has been superseded in this edition.

  • 13.1% presented as the June 2027 ceiling. It is the banked balance accrued to June 2026; the June 2027 increase also includes the unpublished 2027-28 allowance. See F-11.
  • Banking described as capped at three years. Incorrect — there is no cap on the amount banked back to 1 April 1982, and banks do not expire during a tenancy.
  • "24 months or 12 months" banking conflict. The current Rent Board page states the 24-month test; an older rule text suggests 12. The current published page governs practice, and the conservative single-year option is lawful under either reading.
  • Unit 1 anniversary read as 1 April. The April 2026 event was a continuing tenancy, not a new one; the anniversary remains 1 February.
  • "Capped at 7%, so 7% is available." The ordinance sets a 7% statutory maximum on the annual increase, but the published allowance — 1.6% — is the operative number. The maximum is a ceiling on the formula, not an entitlement.
